The Process

Step One!

Send me a note through the contact form with as much detail as you can — what you're after, dates you have in mind, and any location ideas. I'll get back to you within 48 hours with availability and pricing.

Step Two!

We'll lock in a date and talk through the details: where we're shooting, what to wear, and what time the light will be right. A small deposit holds your spot on the calendar.

Step Three!

We shoot. I'll direct as much or as little as you want — mostly I'll keep you talking until you forget I'm there. Your edited gallery arrives within two weeks.
