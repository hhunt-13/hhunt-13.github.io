About

Hi — I'm Hailey.

I'm a student at Southern Nazarene University and a portrait photographer working out of Bethany, Oklahoma.

I picked up a camera because I wanted to keep things — a friend's last semester, a proposal, the ordinary Tuesday that turns out to matter. What kept me shooting is the part nobody warns you about: photography is mostly a people skill. The technical side you can learn in a month. Getting someone to relax in front of a lens takes longer, and it's the part I actually love.

These days I shoot senior sessions and couples on the weekends, portraits for SNU during the semester, and whatever catches my eye when I travel. In May I spent two weeks photographing a documentary series in Costa Rica — doorways, street corners, an ox cart at nine in the morning — which taught me more about patience than any class has.

And this year I ended up on the other side of the camera: I got engaged. Planning a wedding while photographing other people's has made me a better couples photographer — I know exactly how much you want the pictures to be right.

Elevator Pitch

"I'm Hailey Grace — a Southern Nazarene University student and portrait photographer who learned early that the best frame is almost never the posed one."

Over the last two years I've photographed senior sessions, couples sessions, and hundreds of student and faculty portraits for SNU, plus a documentary series in Costa Rica. Along the way I've run the whole operation myself: booking clients, scouting locations, shooting, editing, and delivering galleries on deadline.

What I bring to a team is the same thing I bring to a shoot — I show up early, I make people comfortable fast, and I hit the date I promised. I'm looking for work where visual storytelling and marketing meet, because an audience doesn't stop scrolling for a polished image. They stop for an honest one.
